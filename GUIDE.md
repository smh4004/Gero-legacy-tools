# Dragon Ball Z: Gero's Legacy: Walkthrough and 100% Guide

This guide covers the whole game: the story chapters, every side job and optional area, every item cache, every boss, the four missable memories that unlock the secret chapter, and both endings.

It is regenerated from the game's own data whenever the game changes (`tools/guide.py`). Area names, items, boss stats, the checklist and the area atlas at the end always match the current build.

**Spoilers:** the walkthrough follows the story in order. The checklist, the boss table and the atlas at the end are reference sections.

## Basics
- **Controls:** A talks, examines and attacks up close. B uses your selected skill. L changes the selected skill. Start opens the Status, Journal and Inventory screens.
- **Examine spots:** walk up to the thing, face it, and press A. Caches only give their items once.
- **Journal:** your current objective is always the last Journal entry (Start, then L).
- **Levels:** levelling up raises HP and EP and gives 3 stat points. Spend them on the Status screen ("Use Points"). Strength and defence only go up when you spend points.
- **Items:** a Senzu Bean fully restores HP and energy. Drinks (Milk up to Dinosaur Milk) and Elixirs restore energy (EP). Holy Water boosts your stats for 30 seconds. Exact amounts are in the item table at the end.
- **Saving:** use the save pads (the Capsule Corp logo on the floor) wherever you find them.
- **The secret chapter:** you must find all four of Eighteen's memories in Act 3. See "Missables" below before you start chapter 8.

## Missables
Nothing in the game locks you out of finishing it. Four things in Act 3 decide whether you see the **secret chapter** after the epilogue, and the first two can only be done in chapter 8:
1. **The apple** (chapter 8): visit the old hideout at the docks first, then talk to the fruit seller in West City. 
2. **The mother and son** (chapter 8): after the Vegeta fight, talk to the woman holding her son before you walk up to Tien. Eighteen lets them go. 
3. **Mount Paozu** (chapter 9, Age 772): fly to Goku's house and meet Chi-Chi. 
4. **Kame House** (chapter 9, Age 772): fly to Roshi's island and meet Master Roshi. 

Talking to Eighteen at the Capsule Corporation hub (**Capsule Corporation (Age 772 hub)**) reminds you which of the chapter 9 visits you have made.

# Act One: The Doctor

## Chapter 1: Red Ribbon Science
**You play:** Dr. Gero, then his combat drone. **Goal:** calibrate the drone on the proving ground.

1. The game opens in Gero's lab with his son Gevo. When Gevo leaves, the journal asks you to "Check the drone terminal". Use it (**Main lab (opening)** (examine), **Main lab** (examine)). The technician and the spare drone in the lab have a few lines each.
2. The drone arrives at the **Range Gate**. The briefing gives the objective "Record sensor data at three target sites on the range". The Range Sergeant (**Range Gate**) tells you where the three sites are.
3. **The three target sites.** Each one is a grounded sensor drone; talk to it to log the data:
   - Site A: **Target Field**. From the Range Gate, go up through the Canyon Road.
   - Site B: **Fuel Depot**. From the Range Gate, go down.
   - Site C: **Bone Flats**. From the Range Gate, go right into The Pit, then up.
4. **Caches on the range:**
   - **Fuel Depot** (examine): Soda x2, Tea x1
   - **The Pit** (examine): Milk x2
5. With all three sites logged, the objective becomes "Pass the final test in the live-fire arena". The arena gate on the Bone Flats' west side opens. Before that, examining it just shows the "locked" sign.
6. **Boss: Heavy tank (HP 500, level 58)** in the Live-Fire Arena. 
7. The drone link returns you to the lab for Gero's report, and chapter 2 begins.

## Chapter 2: An Enemy Bullet
**You play:** the drone (from the lab terminal). **Goal:** find Squad Seven, then reach Gevo.

1. Before dawn, Gevo radios in from the Northern Wastelands and the squad is ambushed. Use the drone terminal again.
2. The drone lands on **Ambush Ridge**: "Find the three missing men of Squad Seven". Go up into the Scorched Canyon. It's the hub of this chapter, with exits in every direction.
3. **The three survivors:**
   - Pvt. Kurz: **Fuel Dump**. From the Scorched Canyon, go down.
   - Cpl. Mori: **Raider Cave**. From the Scorched Canyon, go left into the Dry Basin, then up.
   - Sgt. Halden: **Supply Yard**. He's tied up; free him after the boss fight there.
4. **Boss: Raider Chief (HP 480, level 58)** in the Supply Yard. He guards Sgt. Halden.
5. **Caches:**
   - **Fuel Dump** (examine): Soda x2, Milk x1
   - **Supply Yard** (examine): Senzu Bean x1, Tea x1 (face the top crate of the stack)
6. With all three found, the objective becomes "Reach Gevo at the fence on Ambush Ridge". The Scorched Canyon's right-hand exit now leads to Gevo. Walk up to him for the end of the chapter.

## Chapter 3: The Boy Called Goku
**You play:** Gero, and the drone from the lab terminals. **Goal:** save the android blueprints and wipe the archive.

1. Headquarters is under attack: "Recover the android blueprints from the tank room". The technician (**Main lab (under attack)**) reminds you the terminal still controls the drone.
2. In the tank room, take the blueprint core from the pod (**Tank room (under attack)** (examine)). The objective becomes "Wipe the army's archive: south corridor, through the munitions lines". The south corridor from the main lab now leads to the Munitions Lines; before that it only tells you the core comes first.
3. **Munitions Line A, then B, then C.** Looters strip the belts: "Take the vault keycard from the looters' captain".
   - **Mini-boss: Looters' captain (HP 420, level 57)** in Munitions Line C. He drops the vault keycard.
   - Caches: **Munitions Line C** (examine): Tea x1, Milk x2 and **Munitions Line B** (examine): Senzu Bean x1.
4. **Archive Vault** (down from Line C): the blast door needs the keycard. With it, go up into the Army Archive and use the console (**Army Archive** (examine)). Gero wipes the files and starts the self-destruct.
5. The emergency hatch drops you on the escape route: "Get clear of the base". Climb to the old bridge for the end of the chapter.

## Chapter 4: The First Androids
**You play:** the drone (Gero at the end). **Goal:** restore the bunker's power and wake Android 16.

1. Years later, in the northern bunker: "Restore power: three relays, one in the bunker and two outside".
2. **Relay 1:** **Northern bunker** (examine).
3. Leave the bunker by its lower exit for the Mountain Pass. Prototypes Gero dumped here still have power.
   - **Relay 2:** **Generator Cave**. Go right from the Mountain Pass.
   - **Relay 3:** **Old Mine**. Go up from the Generator Cave, through the hole at the top of its east ramp.
   - **Mini-boss: Prototype 9 (HP 450, level 59)** sits on relay 3 in the Old Mine; destroy it first.
   - The barred shaft in the Old Mine is not an exit (**Old Mine (768)** (examine), **Old Mine** (examine)).
   - Caches: **Generator Cave** (examine): Soda x2, Senzu Bean x1 and **Old Mine** (examine): Tea x1, Milk x1.
4. With all three relays on, Prototype 12 breaks out of the conversion pod: "Destroy Prototype 12".
5. **Boss: Prototype 12 (HP 420, level 53)** in the bunker incubator.
6. Talk to Android 16 in the tank room (**Bunker tank room**). Then enter the conversion pod (**Bunker incubator** (examine)): "Enter the conversion pod in the incubator". **End of Act One.**

# Act Two: Lapis and Lazuli

## Chapter 5: Siblings
**You play:** Lapis (Lazuli in the party). **Goal:** West City. Four things must be done before the chapter ends: the fence, Hercule, the police chase, and the warehouse.

1. West City: "Explore West City". The townsfolk all have something to say.
2. **The fence** (**West City**) recognises the Red Ribbon mark on your stolen capsule case: "Find out who's asking about you".
3. **Young Hercule** (**West City**) is practising his kicks on the sidewalk. Talk to him.
4. **"Ditch the Patrol":** talk to the police officer (**West City**): "Lose the cop in the park". Lose him behind the park hedge, on the left side of West City. Afterwards, the man in the long coat (**West City**) has a new line.
5. **The Red Fangs:** the street vendor in the Circus Lot (**Circus Lot**) says the gang took your warehouse: "Take back the warehouse from the Red Fangs". Go down from West City to the Riverside, right to the Docks, then up to the Old Warehouse.
   - **Boss: Big Ox, Red Fang boss (HP 420, level 55)** and his gang in the Old Warehouse.
   - Then take your tin box and the gang's haul (**Old Warehouse** (examine): Senzu Bean x1, Soda x2).
6. **Return the stolen goods.** These are optional and only work after the warehouse:
   - The Z-Mart clerk (**Z-Mart**: V. Soda x2).
   - The West City Museum curator (**West City Museum**: Senzu Bean x1). The Red Ribbon exhibit in the museum (**West City Museum** (examine)) is worth a look.
7. **Other caches:** **Riverside** (examine): Tea x1 and **Docks** (hold Up): Milk x1, C. Soda x1 (the dome house).
8. Once the fence, Hercule, the chase and the warehouse are done, the chapter ends: "Hide out until nightfall; Get to the docks unseen".

## Chapter 6: Taken
**You play:** Lapis, at night. **Goal:** get home unseen.

1. West City at night. A drone sweeps the crossroads; go down the south road to the Riverside.
2. Gero's hunter drones patrol the Riverside and the Docks.
   - Caches: **Riverside (night)** (examine): Root Beer x1 and **Docks (night)** (hold Up): Senzu Bean x1.
3. The Old Warehouse: "Destroy the machine in the warehouse".
4. **Boss: The Collector (HP 520, level 56)**.
5. The ambush follows. The twins wake in Gero's lab: "Lapis and Lazuli are gone". You also unlock the **Outfit** skill: select it and press B to switch between the android outfit and the old clothes.

# Act Three: Seventeen and Eighteen

## Chapter 7: Activation
**You play:** Seventeen (Eighteen in the party). **Goal:** get out of Gero's hidden laboratory.

1. Age 767. Gero wakes the twins, and it goes badly for him: "Leave Dr. Gero's hidden laboratory". Eighteen lingers in the pod room.
2. Go down into the Security Wing. The lab's computer locks everything down: "Get through the lockdown to the control room".
   - Cache: **Security Wing** (examine): Senzu Bean x1, Tea x1.
3. **Boss: Security Unit (HP 3300, level 56)** guards the Control Room (right of the Security Wing).
4. Use the console (**Control Room** (examine)) to end the lockdown. Then leave through the cave to the northern mountains for the end of the chapter.

## Chapter 8: The Massacre
**You play:** Seventeen; one fight as Eighteen. **Goal:** West City, then the Z Fighters.

1. West City, three days later: "Visit the old hideout at the docks". Townsfolk: one man has heard that Goku is dead.
2. Go down to the Riverside, right to the Docks, and up to the Old Warehouse. Gero's old machines prowl here.
   - **The tin box** (needed for the apple). Examine the crates in the Old Warehouse (**Old Warehouse (767)** (examine)): "Head back into West City".
   - Caches: **Riverside (767)** (examine): C. Soda x1 and **Docks (767)** (hold Up): Elixir x1, Senzu Bean x1.
3. **Memory: the apple (missable).** Back in West City, talk to the fruit seller (**West City (Age 767)**). It only works after the tin box. The objective becomes "Meet them at the crossroads".
4. Walk onto the crossroads; the Z Fighters land.
5. **Boss: Vegeta (HP 1100, level 54)**.
6. After Vegeta, **talk to the woman and her son before anything else** (**Crossroads: after Vegeta**). This is missable memory 2. Then walk up to Tien. Tien's and Yamcha's stands are scenes.
7. **Boss: Krillin (HP 700, level 53)**, fought as Eighteen. Krillin remembers whether you spared the mother and son.
8. **Boss: Piccolo (HP 1350, level 55)**, fought as Seventeen. Gohan runs, and the night on the Z-Mart roof ends the chapter.

## Chapter 9: Years of Terror
**You play:** Seventeen. **Goal:** Gero's computer, Super 13, then the open world. Two missable memories are in this chapter.

1. Age 768, Capsule Corporation: "Search Capsule Corporation". Bulma and baby Trunks are in the dome house (**Capsule Corporation (Age 768)**); talk to her. Gero's computer calls the twins home.
2. **The northern mountains.** The bunker door is sealed: "Destroy the Sentinel in the generator cave".
   - **Mini-boss: The Sentinel (HP 3700, level 58)** in the Generator Cave (right of the Mountain Pass).
   - Cache: **Old Mine (768)** (examine): Hercule-ade x2, Senzu Bean x1. The Old Mine is up from the Generator Cave.
   - With the Sentinel destroyed, the bunker door on the left of the Mountain Pass opens.
3. The bunker: "Find Unit Thirteen". Android 16's empty pod is in the tank room (**Bunker tank room (Age 768)** (examine)).
4. **Boss: Super Android 13 (HP 1700, level 58)** in the incubator.
5. **Age 772: the hub.** Capsule Corporation is now the twins' base: "Fly anywhere you like: the airship pad or the hangar. West City's crossroads when you're ready.".
   - **The world map opens here.** Examine Bulma's airship, or walk off the west edge of the grounds, or examine the hangar jets to take off. See "The open world" below.
   - **Memory: Mount Paozu (missable).** Land on Mount Paozu and meet Chi-Chi (**Mount Paozu: Goku's house** (walk in)).
   - **Memory: Kame House (missable).** Land on Roshi's island and meet Roshi (**Kame House** (walk in)).
6. When you're ready, walk out of the hub's front gate to West City.
7. **Boss: Gohan (HP 1600, level 57)** (Age 774), in the West City ruins. Then "End of Chapter 9".

## Chapter 10: The Last Stand
**You play:** Seventeen. **Goal:** the crossroads.

1. Age 780, the Circus Lot ruins: "Get to the crossroads". The resistance has wired the lot with turrets and old machines.
   - Cache: **Circus Lot ruins (780)** (examine): Senzu Bean x1, Elixir x1.
   - The world map is open again here: talk to Eighteen, or walk off the top of the lot.
2. Go right to the crossroads in the rain. If you visited Mount Paozu, Eighteen tells Gohan about his mother.
3. **Final boss: One-armed Future Gohan (HP 1850, level 58)**.
4. **The finale** (Age 785) and the **epilogue** (Age 788) follow. With all four memories, the **secret chapter** starts. Otherwise the game ends and hints that something else could wake up.

# The open world (Act 3, from chapter 9)
**Taking off:** Bulma's airship or the hangar jets at the Age 772 hub, Eighteen at any destination, or walking off the edge of an open area. The first take-off explains the controls.

**Flying:** steer with the + pad. Hold A over a place to land on it. Kami's Lookout floats high above the wasteland, so hold B to climb into it. West City lands at the Age 772 hub, or at the Age 780 Circus Lot from chapter 10.

Eighteen stands next to the landing spot at every destination; talk to her to fly on.

**The seven stone Dragon Balls.** Kami died in Age 767, so the Dragon Balls have turned to stone. Find all seven, then take them to Kami's Lookout:
1. **One-star:** the wreck of Kame House (**Kame House** (examine): Dragon Ball 1 x1).
2. **Two-star:** Hercule in Orange Star City (**Orange Star City**: Dragon Ball 2 x1).
3. **Three-star:** under Prototype 11 in Diablo Desert (**Diablo Desert** (examine): Dragon Ball 3 x1). You must beat **Prototype 11 (HP 6150, level 60)** first (optional boss).
4. **Four-star:** the shrine in Goku's house on Mount Paozu (**Mount Paozu: Goku's house** (examine): Dragon Ball 4 x1).
5. **Five-star:** the elder of Nataday Village (**Nataday Village**: Dragon Ball 5 x1, Senzu Bean x1). Run Butcher's raiders out first: **Butcher, raider boss (HP 4000, level 59)** (optional boss). The farmer (**Nataday Village**) tells you about them.
6. **Six-star:** Pilaf's old safe (**Pilaf's Castle** (examine): Dragon Ball 6 x1). Beat **Ninja captain (HP 3550, level 59)** first (optional boss).
7. **Seven-star:** the tournament announcer on Papaya Island (**Papaya Island**: Dragon Ball 7 x1). The welded tournament gate is **Papaya Island** (examine).

**Kami's palace:** stand under the palace arch on the Lookout and examine it (**Kami's Lookout** (examine): Holy Water x2, Senzu Bean x3, DinosaurMilk x1). With all seven balls, Eighteen sets them on the altar.

**Other sights:** Orange Star City's school (**Orange Star City** (examine)), a police officer (**Orange Star City**) and a schoolgirl (**Orange Star City**). Every place also has its own first-landing scene.

# Secret Chapter: First Steps
**Unlocked by:** all four memories (see "Missables"). **You play:** Imperfect Cell, Age 788. Cell has a fixed level and gains no EXP.

1. Cell wakes beneath the bunker: "Find out what happened". Android 16's pod is in the tank room (**Bunker tank room (Age 788)** (examine)).
2. In the main lab, the backup console plays Gero's recording (**Bunker main lab (Age 788)** (examine)): "Find Trunks and his time machine".
3. The south corridor leads to the Age 788 Security Wing. It holds four Cyborgs (Cyborg (HP 875, level 90)); Cell absorbs each one. The hangar door (**Security Wing, Age 788** (walk in)) leads outside.
4. **Boss: Future Trunks, Age 788 (HP 1700, level 60)** in the West City ruins.
5. Cell takes the time machine to Age 763: "Find somewhere to hide". Burrow into the soft ground of the clearing to end the secret chapter.
